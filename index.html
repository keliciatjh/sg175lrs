Role Encyclopedia & Official Guidelines

🐺 Werewolf

Wakes up during the night phase with fellow wolves to vote and eliminate one target player.

🔮 Seer

Investigates one player's alignment per night. Learns whether the player is Good or a Werewolf.

🧪 Witch

Holds one Healing potion and one Poison potion. Can only use one potion per night phase.

🛡️ Guard

Protects one player each night from Werewolf kills. Cannot protect the same target two nights in a row.

🎯 Hunter

When eliminated by werewolf attack or day vote, can shoot and instantly eliminate any target player.

👨‍🌾 Villager

No special night abilities. Relies on logic, observation, and voting power to exile Werewolves.
